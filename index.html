Event Invitation Generator

Personalised WhatsApp invitations from your Excel guest list, one guest at a time.

What should my Excel guest list look like?

Put the column headers on row 2 of the first sheet; guests start on row 3. The easiest start is Download blank guest list template.

ColumnWhat it's for
Addressable NamesRequiredThe greeting, e.g. “Ravi & Priya”. It's {name} in the message.
Add to Invite List?RequiredYes to include the guest (Y, TRUE and ✓ also work).
NameOptionalTheir name as saved in WhatsApp, copied for searching.
CategoryOptionalFamily, Friends, Work… to send in groups.
Invited?, Invited OnOptionalFilled in for you when you mark a guest as sent.
Any other columnOptionalUse it in the message as {Column name}, e.g. {Adults}.

Everything stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Event details

Fill these in once: they replace the matching [blanks] in the message, such as [Date] and [Address], for every guest.

The links are added at the cursor as [Map link] and [Calendar link], and filled in from the address, date and time.

Select text and use the toolbar. When you paste from Word, Google Docs or email, bold, italic and strikethrough are kept; other styling is dropped (WhatsApp can't show it).
